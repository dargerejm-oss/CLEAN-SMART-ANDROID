4	How does it work?
5	1. Is your phone full?
6	Duplicate photos, failed shots, huge videos, useless emails… all take space.
7	2. Clean Smart searches for you
8	One single button. Nothing is deleted during the scan.
9	3. You choose
10	What is safe is already ticked for you. For the rest, you decide.
11	4. Nothing is lost
12	Everything goes through the bin: 30 days to get a photo or an email back.
13	5. Everything stays safe
14	Backup to an SD card, USB drive or Google Drive, even on its own at night.
15	Complete clean-up (recommended)
16	The home screen has a big green "Complete clean-up" tile and 3 doors: 📸 Photos, 🧹 Phone, ✉️ Emails. The complete clean-up does everything in the right order; the GREEN button is always the next step.
17	Safe clean-up: extra duplicates and bursts (the best one is kept), black or white photos, old screenshots. One button.
18	Check the doubtful ones: slightly blurry photos, WhatsApp… Swipe left to discard, right to keep (it becomes a favourite).
19	Keep them safe: only GOOD photos are backed up. Favourites also go to "Mes meilleures" (My best).
20	Free up space: old photos already backed up leave the phone.
21	NEW 2.0 — And your emails: your mailbox space, newsletters and large emails, then the guided mailbox clean-up. At Google, emails and Google Photos share the same 15 GB.
22	The summary shows what is freed on the phone AND in the mailbox. In bursts, the photo where nobody has their eyes closed is kept.
23	Junk files (NEW 2.0)
24	Temporary files, thumbnails, cache folders, empty folders, installation files, leftovers of uninstalled apps. Photos, videos and music are never touched.
25	Just once: allow "All files access". The search starts by itself.
26	Safe groups are ticked: they are recreated automatically or no longer needed. "Clean" and you're done.
27	"To check" groups (other .apk, app leftovers, old downloads) go to the Clean Smart bin: "Put everything back" for 7 days.
28	Other apps' cache: Android forbids apps from clearing it since Android 11. The button opens Android's "Free up space" screen.
29	📱 My apps: the biggest ones and those not opened for 3 months. Tap one to uninstall it or clear its cache.
30	Health score and Sunday clean-up (NEW 2.0)
31	On the home screen, a score from 0 to 100 sums up the phone: free space, backup, photos to sort, junk files, mailbox. It also tells you what to do to earn points.
32	In Options › Automatic, turn on "Automatic Sunday clean-up": safe junk files and spam are removed automatically, a notification sums it up.
33	Photos never leave on their own: the notification says how many to sort, you tap and choose.
34	Simpler options: Backup, Automatic, Display. The rest is in "Advanced settings".
35	Leave Google Photos
36	All your Google Photos on a USB drive or hard disk, sorted by year, without duplicates. One single dashboard: the "MAINTENANT" (now) step is open, with the green button.
37	Space: note the size of Google Photos, Clean Smart works out the space to plan.
38	The disk: plug in the USB drive or disk (USB-C adapter) and choose a folder on it.
39	Ask Google for a copy: the model shows exactly what to tick (.zip files of 2 GB).
40	The files: the 001, 002… list tells you which one to download. "Traiter un fichier", then delete it. A notification tells you when it is done.
41	Each photo is sorted by year, duplicates and extra bursts are left out, everything is re-read and checked. The gauge shows how much is safe.
42	Problem (phone or disk full, damaged file): nothing is lost, run the same file again. "Si ça bloque" button.
43	NEW 2.0 — "Fully automatic": allow "All files access" once, start, then just tap the links in Google's email. Each file is copied as soon as it arrives in Download, then deleted from the phone once verified.
44	Free up Google: the button only appears when the copy is complete, phone backup is off and you have checked your photos on the drive. You do the deleting; Google's bin keeps everything for 60 days.
45	"Check the drive" re-reads every copied photo and reports any damaged one. Every 2 months, a notification reminds you to request a new copy: only new photos are added.
46	Get my iCloud photos (former iPhone)
47	For iPhone photos: the same dashboard as Google Photos, with Apple's official export.
48	Space: on the iPhone, Settings › your name › iCloud. Clean Smart works out the space to plan.
49	The disk: plug in the USB drive or disk and choose a folder on it.
50	On privacy.apple.com: "Request a copy of your data", tick "iCloud Photos", files of 1 or 2 GB.
51	Apple sends an email within a few days. Download "Part 1", process it, delete it, then "Part 2"…
52	Everything is sorted by year in "iCloud Photos", without duplicates, the best photo of each burst kept.

180	Frequently asked questions
181	Are my photos deleted right away?
182	No. They go to the Gallery bin: 30 days to restore them, or "I made a mistake".
183	Do my photos go on the Internet?
184	No. Everything happens on the phone. Only the backup goes where you chose (SD card, USB, Drive).
185	Why an app password?
186	Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud require it for mail apps. It stays encrypted on your phone.
187	The space does not come back after cleaning?
188	The bin keeps photos for 30 days. Tap "Empty the bin now" on the home screen.
189	I erased my copy on the disk, nothing is copied any more?
190	In the Google Photos or iCloud Photos tab, tap "Start over (I erased my copy)" at the bottom of the screen, then run your ZIP files again.
191	How do I get the latest version?
192	When a new version exists, an orange banner appears on the home screen: tap it, then open the downloaded file. Or Options > "Rechercher une mise à jour" (Check for updates).
